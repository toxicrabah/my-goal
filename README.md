# 🎯 MyGoal — Objective & Quest Tracker

**MyGoal** is a clean, feature-packed objective and streak tracker designed to help you break down big targets into manageable milestone steps, build daily consistency, and celebrate your wins.

![License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/toxicrabah/my-goal?color=6366f1)
![HTML5](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20with-HTML5%20%2F%20CSS3%20%2F%20JS-orange)

---

## ✨ Features

* 🚀 **Milestone Tracking:** Break down any goal into actionable steps with dynamic progress bars.
* 🔥 **Streak Counter:** Keeps track of your daily check-ins automatically to build long-term habits.
* 🎉 **Confetti Celebrations:** Custom confetti cannon effect triggers whenever you complete 100% of a goal.
* 💡 **Daily Motivation:** Rotates inspirational quotes daily to keep your momentum going.
* 💾 **Local Data Persistence:** All your goals and streak stats save automatically in your browser—no account creation required.
* 💬 **Feedback System:** Built-in form to send feedback and suggestions straight to the developer.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **HTML5** & **CSS3** (Custom glassmorphism dark mode design)
* **JavaScript (Vanilla JS)** (Confetti engine, streak logic, and `localStorage`)
* **Formspree** (Direct email feedback integration)
